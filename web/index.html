                  Correct a whole network

                  Puts the stops and relations already mapped in OSM in the form of this site, without changing a trace. You get a file to review.

                  Load a feed on the Converter page first: it gives the networks and their area.

                  A mass edit of what others have mapped. Every stop and relation of the network may change, including stops shared with other networks.
                  • Stops: name without the town, official_name with it, punctuation, no alt_name, bus=yes on the platforms of bus and coach lines, GTFS ids and network tags tidied. A stop_position keeps only its vehicle and name: the rest goes to its platform. A platform tagged as a stop_position, or the reverse, is put right from where it stands.
                  • Route and route_master relations: name, from, to, via, and the roles stop and platform of the members.
                  • Unchanged: ways, the order of the members, positions.

                  Nothing is sent to OpenStreetMap: you get an .osm file. Before uploading it, review it in JOSM and follow the Automated Edits code of conduct: document the edit and discuss it with the local community first.

                  Allow about one second per stop on the first run: towns come from Nominatim, one request per second at most.

                  Twisto and all the Normandy transport networks of Atoumod were the pioneer GTFS feeds in testing this site.
